---
title: "People vs. Talents"
description: "How MuviDB separates its public filmography encyclopedia (/people) from bookable Pro profiles (/talents)."
---

## `/people` — Public Encyclopedia

Every actor, director, writer, producer, and crew member credited in a film or stage play has a public profile at `/people/:slug`. These pages aggregate verified screen credits, awards, biographies, and media.

## `/talents` — Bookable Industry Talents

When a professional claims their profile or applies for a verified **Pro Talent** listing, they appear in `/talents` with:

- Availability status (`Available`, `Booked`, `On Set`)
- Primary craft, skills, and union/guild affiliations
- Direct representation / booking contacts
- Showreels and portfolio links
